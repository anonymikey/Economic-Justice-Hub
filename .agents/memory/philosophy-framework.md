EJF's current philosophy is built on a **People → Economic Systems → Human Dignity → Economic Justice** framework. The key affirmations are:

- Human dignity is the foundation of Economic Justice.
- Economic rights are fundamental human rights.
- People are the purpose of every economy.
- Equity, Justice and Prosperity are the enduring guiding principles.
- Economic Justice is achieved by **transforming economic systems**, not treating their symptoms.
- Natural wealth is a strategic public asset whose governance should enable people and communities to achieve Economic Justice while safeguarding future generations.
- Institutions are the architects of economic systems; their quality determines how rights are protected, opportunities are created and prosperity is shared.

The principal intellectual tools are:

- **The Economic Justice Doctrine™** — the principles through which EJF understands, interprets and advances Economic Justice.
- **The Natural Wealth Lens™** — the distinctive perspective on the relationship between people, natural wealth, institutions and prosperity.
- **The iMagine Model™ / iMagine Framework™** — the transformation methodology: Discover → Reimagine → Design → Implement → Learn → Transform.

**Why:** The old website language centered on "Natural Wealth fulfils its highest purpose through Economic Justice." The HQ documents reframe this around people, economic systems and human dignity, and they introduce the Doctrine, Lens and iMagine tools as EJF's defining intellectual contribution. Using the old framing is now a content error.

**How to apply:** On the Our Philosophy page, lead with "What We Believe," present the Doctrine principles, show the iMagine Model as the transformation path, and explain the Natural Wealth Lens as the distinctive analytical perspective. Remove the old "Natural Wealth fulfils its highest purpose" wording from any active page.
